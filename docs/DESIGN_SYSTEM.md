# Design System — tokens (proposal)

_Palette generated by [`scripts/palette.py`](../scripts/palette.py) in OKLCH from the brand anchors, with WCAG contrast measured. Everything here becomes CSS variables in `frontend/app/styles/tokens.css` and is mapped into Tailwind in Phase 2._

## 1. Brand anchor

The **logo and the physical signage are magenta `#F51B97`**. The legacy site's cyan, yellow and navy came with the purchased template. My proposal is:

- **Primary = Expressions Magenta**: the brand, used for CTAs, active states and highlights.
- **Secondary = Clinical Teal**, derived from the legacy cyan `#00A3C8`. It gives calm and a "medical" feel, and is used for informational UI, icons and the 3D material tint.
- **Neutrals = cool slate**, with a soft blue undertone so it sits calmly against magenta.
- The template yellow is **dropped**.

> ⚠️ `#F51B97` on white is **3.83:1**, which fails AA for body text. It is fine for large text (≥ 24px), icons and decoration. **Buttons and links use primary-600 `#D2027F` (5.18:1 with white text)**, and body-sized text links use primary-700 `#AD0068` (7.05:1).

## 3. Typography

- **Headings: "Plus Jakarta Sans"** (variable, 600–800). It is warm, modern and premium, and has more character than Poppins at about half the payload when subset.
- **Body: "Inter"** (variable, 400–600), which has excellent legibility at small sizes and for numerals in the admin.
- Alternative if the client wants continuity: Poppins headings + Nunito body (the legacy pair).
- Self-hosted woff2, Latin subset (+ Devanagari subset later for Hindi/Marathi/Konkani), `font-display: swap`, heading font preloaded, with size-adjusted system fallbacks to avoid CLS.

## 4. Space, radius, elevation, motion

- **Spacing:** 4px base. Tokens 1–24 (4→96px). Section padding is clamp(4rem, 8vw, 8rem).
- **Container:** max 1240px; 16px gutter on mobile, 24px on tablet, 32px on desktop.
- **Radius:** `sm` 8 · `md` 12 · `lg` 16 (inputs, buttons) · `xl` 24 (cards) · `2xl` 32 (hero media) · `full` (pills, tab-bar Book button).
- **Elevation** uses soft, tinted shadows (cool slate at 6–12% opacity, never pure black): `e1` cards, `e2` hover/sticky, `e3` sheets/modals.
- **Glass:** reserved for the mobile bottom tab bar and the floating sticky CTA. Background is `--bg` at 72% with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(1.4)`, with a solid fallback.
- **Motion:** 150ms (press), 200ms (hover/colour), 250ms (sheets, page transitions), 300ms max for anything larger. Easing is `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)` (ease-out), and springs for sheets (stiffness 400, damping 40). `prefers-reduced-motion` turns transforms into opacity-only fades and stops all auto-play and 3D rotation.

## 6. Art direction

- **Photography first:** the clinic's real photos of Dr. Sandeep and the microscope work are warm and authentic. They get a consistent light grade with a gentle magenta-teal split tone, rounded 24–32px masks, and generous whitespace.
- **Illustration:** a minimal line-icon set (Lucide plus a few custom dental glyphs: tooth, implant, braces, aligner), stroke 1.75px, in the secondary colour.
- **3D:** a stylised tooth or implant with a frosted-ceramic (pearl white) material, a teal subsurface tint and a magenta rim light, so the 3D section reads as on-brand rather than stock.
- **No stock photos of smiling models.** Real clinic only, or nothing.
