Cosmetic Treatments

Smile Designing

It is a SYSTEM of changing size,shape,color and alignment of teeth and altering associated tissues in order to enhance one’s apperance.

Laminate Veneers

These are translucent, thin laminates made to improve theesthetics(shape, spacing, color)of natural teeth, to correct crowding or to replace composite bonding.

Ceramic Crowns

Cosmetic crowns are made from translucent ceramic materials which is attractive to look at and blends in well with the rest of your teeth.

Cad Cam Dental Crowns

Computer Aided Design & Computer Assissted Milling helps to design & manufacture different types of crowns and dental restoration.

Gum Shaping

Gum depigmentation/Shaping (aka gum bleaching) is a procedure used in cosmetic dentistry to remove black spots or patches on the gums caused by excessive melanin.

Dental Bleaching

Dental Bleaching/Tooth Whitening is a very effective way of lightening natural colour of your tooth without removing any of the tooth surface.

Tooth Jewellery

We use Tooth jewellery that is accredited & specifically designed to be bonded to teeth. It can be easily removed/changed without damaging the tooth.
